# WoW Forever Enhancement Shaman Gameplay Sim

A browser sim for practising the level 60 Enhancement Shaman rotation in World of Warcraft: Forever. You press the abilities; the sim auto-attacks a raid boss dummy, tracks mana, procs and cooldowns, and shows a damage breakdown when the dummy dies.

**Play it:** https://wowf-gameplay.github.io/shaman-gameplay-sim/

## Features

- Stormstrike, Earth Shock, Flame Shock, Fire Nova, Lightning Bolt, Searing Totem, Rage of the Farseer, Blood Fury, Major Mana Potion and Demonic Rune
- Maelstrom Weapon stacks for instant, free Lightning Bolts; Flurry, Elemental Devastation, Clearcasting, Crusader and Windfury Totem procs
- Two-hander with Rockbiter Weapon, with Mental Dexterity and Mental Quickness turning Intellect into attack power and spell damage
- Toggles for raid buffs, consumables and boss debuffs
- Gear and talent tabs, plus a Stats tab for entering your own character stats
- Rebindable keys (with Shift/Ctrl), adjustable dummy health, and a Warcraft Logs-style end-of-fight breakdown

## Setup

The default gear, enchants and 18/33/0 talent build follow the [MythicSim](https://mythicsim.com/wow-forever/tier-list) Enhancement Shaman preset. Mechanics follow the open-source engine MythicSim is built on and Forever beta tooltips; a few values are fitted to MythicSim's results, and everything may change as the beta does.

## Running locally

It is a single file with no build step: open `index.html` in a browser. Ability icons load from Wowhead's image server, so they need an internet connection.
